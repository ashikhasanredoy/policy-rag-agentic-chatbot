# Comprehensive Annual Leave, Statutory Time Off, and Absence Management Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-001  
**Category:** Human Resources  
**Department:** Global People & Culture  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Status:** Active  
**Applies To:** All Full-time, Part-time, and Fixed-Term Employees  

---

## Section 1: Purpose, Objectives, and Scope
1.1 **Purpose**: This policy establishes comprehensive guidelines governing paid annual vacation leave, public holidays, statutory leaves of absence, medical leave, parental support, and unpaid sabbaticals.  
1.2 **Philosophy**: The organization recognizes that rest, recuperation, and personal time are vital for sustained employee well-being, cognitive health, and operational excellence.  
1.3 **Scope**: Applicable to all regular employees across corporate headquarters, regional offices, and remote workforce hubs.

---

## Section 2: Annual Leave Entitlements and Accrual Schedule
2.1 **Full-Time Permanent Employees**: Regular full-time employees accrue **twenty (20) days of paid annual leave** per full calendar year (January 1 to December 31).  
2.2 **Monthly Accrual Rate**: Leave accrues on the last day of each completed calendar month of active service at a rate of **1.66 working days per month** (13.33 hours/month).  
2.3 **Tenure-Based Leave Increments**: Employees with continuous service milestones receive enhanced annual leave allocations:
- **0 to 2 Years of Service**: 20 days per annum (1.66 days/month).
- **3 to 4 Years of Service**: 23 days per annum (1.91 days/month).
- **5+ Years of Service**: 25 days per annum (2.08 days/month).  
2.4 **Part-Time Employees**: Part-time staff receive pro-rated leave allocations calculated proportionately to their contracted weekly hours compared to standard 40-hour full-time schedules.  
2.5 **Probationary Access**: New hires begin accruing leave from Day 1 of employment; accrued days may be requested during the 90-day probationary period subject to manager approval.

---

## Section 3: Carry-Over, Year-End Rollover, and Expiration Rules
3.1 **Maximum Rollover Allowance**: Employees are encouraged to utilize their full annual leave allocation within the accrued calendar year. A maximum of **five (5) unused annual leave days** may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.  
3.2 **Mandatory Rollover Expiration**: Any carried-over leave days must be fully taken before **March 31st** of the subsequent year. Any remaining carried-over days as of April 1st will automatically expire and will be forfeited without financial compensation.  
3.3 **No Cash-Out (Encashment) Option**: To incentivize genuine employee rest, active employees cannot cash out or sell back unused annual leave days for salary, except upon formal termination or resignation of employment as required by law.

---

## Section 4: Booking Procedures, Notice Periods, and Approval Matrix
4.1 **Self-Service Booking**: All leave requests must be formally submitted and tracked through the Human Resources Information System (HRIS / Workday). Verbal or informal off-system arrangements are strictly void.  
4.2 **Required Advance Notice Windows**:
- **Single Day Absence (1-2 days)**: Minimum two (2) business days advance submission.
- **Short Leave (3-5 consecutive days)**: Minimum five (5) business days advance submission.
- **Extended Leave (6+ consecutive days)**: Minimum ten (10) business days advance submission.  
4.3 **Manager Discretion and Blackout Windows**: Managers must respond to leave requests within three (3) business days. Approvals may take into account critical operational cycles (e.g. quarterly financial closes or major product releases); however, arbitrary denials are prohibited.  
4.4 **Public Holidays Coincidence**: If an approved annual leave day falls on a recognized national or company public holiday, that day will not be deducted from the employee's annual leave balance.

---

## Section 5: Paid Sick Leave and Medical Certification
5.1 **Sick Leave Allocation**: Full-time employees receive **ten (10) paid sick and wellness days per calendar year**, available from the first day of employment.  
5.2 **Notification Protocol**: In the event of sudden illness, the employee must inform their direct manager via Slack or email by 9:00 AM local time on each morning of absence.  
5.3 **Doctor's Note (Medical Certificate)**: A certified medical practitioner's certificate is required for continuous sick absences exceeding **three (3) consecutive working days**, or for any sick day taken immediately adjacent to a scheduled annual leave or public holiday.  
5.4 **Mental Health Days**: Sick leave days may be utilized for mental health, psychological recuperation, and personal caregiver support.

---

## Section 6: Parental Leave (Maternity, Paternity, Adoption)
6.1 **Primary Caregiver (Maternity Leave)**: Eligible full-time employees with at least 6 months of continuous service receive **sixteen (16) weeks of 100% fully paid maternity/primary caregiver leave** following the birth, adoption, or surrogacy of a child.  
6.2 **Secondary Caregiver (Paternity Leave)**: Secondary caregivers receive **six (6) weeks of 100% fully paid paternity leave**, which may be taken continuously or in two distinct blocks within the child's first 12 months.  
6.3 **Benefits Continuity**: Full medical, dental, life insurance, and pension contributions continue uninterrupted throughout all parental leave periods.

---

## Section 7: Compassionate (Bereavement) and Civic Leaves
7.1 **Immediate Family Bereavement**: Employees are granted **five (5) days of fully paid bereavement leave** for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or grandchild).  
7.2 **Extended Relative Bereavement**: Up to **two (2) days of paid leave** is provided for the death of an extended relative (aunt, uncle, in-law, first cousin).  
7.3 **Jury Duty & Civic Service**: Up to ten (10) business days of paid leave per calendar year is provided for compulsory jury summons or court witness appearances upon provision of the official court notice.  
7.4 **Voting Leave**: Up to two (2) consecutive hours of paid time off is provided on official national election voting days if work schedules prevent polling access.

---

## Section 8: Unpaid Personal Leaves and Extended Sabbaticals
8.1 **Unpaid Personal Leave**: Employees may request up to thirty (30) calendar days of unpaid leave for emergency personal reasons with Department Head approval.  
8.2 **Career Sabbatical Program**: Employees with at least **three (3) consecutive years of tenure** in good standing may apply for an unpaid sabbatical ranging from **one (1) to three (3) months** for academic research, community volunteerism, or skill development. Job role and seniority level are guaranteed upon return.

---

## Section 9: Resignation, Termination, and Leave Settlement
9.1 **Final Settlement Calculation**: Upon termination of employment (voluntary resignation or involuntary termination), accrued but unused annual leave days up to the final date of service are paid out in the final paycheck calculated at the base daily rate.  
9.2 **Excess Leave Recovery**: If an employee has taken more leave than accrued pro-rata at the time of departure, the monetary value of the unaccrued deficit will be deducted from the final compensation payout.
