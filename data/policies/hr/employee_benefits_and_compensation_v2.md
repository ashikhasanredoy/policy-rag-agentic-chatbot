**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Status:** Active  
**Applies To:** All Regular Full-time and Part-time Employees  

---

## Section 1: Total Rewards Strategy and Eligibility
1.1 **Overview**: The organization provides a comprehensive, competitive Total Rewards program designed to support the physical, mental, financial, and professional well-being of our global workforce.  
1.2 **Eligibility Date**: Full-time regular employees and their eligible dependents (spouse, domestic partner, and dependent children up to age 26) are eligible for all health, retirement, and wellness benefits starting on the **first day of the calendar month following date of hire**.

---

## Section 2: Healthcare, Dental, and Vision Coverage
2.1 **Medical Insurance Plans**: The company offers three tier options:
- **PPO Premium Plan**: Low deductible, comprehensive national provider network, company covers 85% of employee premiums and 70% of dependent premiums.
- **High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA**: Paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). The company contributes an annual seed of **$1,000.00 USD for individuals** or **$2,000.00 USD for family coverage**.
- **HMO Plan**: Fully integrated local provider network with zero copay preventive care.  
2.2 **Comprehensive Dental Coverage**: 100% coverage for preventive cleanings twice yearly, 80% coverage for basic dental restorative procedures, and 50% coverage for major dental and orthodontic services up to an annual maximum of $2,500.00 per covered individual.  
2.3 **Vision Care**: Annual comprehensive eye exams are covered with a $10 copay. An annual frame and contact lens hardware allowance of **$250.00 USD** is provided per calendar year.

---

## Section 3: Retirement Savings Plan (401k / Pension Match)
3.1 **401(k) Matching Structure**: The company offers an immediate matching contribution on traditional pre-tax and Roth 401(k) employee deferrals:
- **Company Match**: **50% matching contribution on employee contributions up to 6% of eligible base salary** (maximum corporate contribution of 3% of base salary).  
3.2 **Automatic Enrollment**: New eligible employees are automatically enrolled at a 4% default contribution rate 30 days after onboarding, with the option to opt-out or adjust contribution rates at any time.  
3.3 **Vesting Schedule**: Safe-harbor matching contributions vest immediately at **100% on Day 1**.

---

## Section 4: Professional Development and Tuition Reimbursement
4.1 **Annual Tuition Assistance**: Full-time employees with at least twelve (12) months of continuous service may apply for tuition reimbursement up to **$5,250.00 USD per calendar year** (tax-free under IRS Section 127) for accredited undergraduate, graduate, or certified professional credential programs relevant to their career trajectory.  
4.2 **Grade Requirement**: Reimbursement requires a minimum grade of "B" (3.0 GPA) or a "Pass" in pass/fail accredited courses.  
4.3 **Retention Commitment**: Employees receiving tuition reimbursement must agree to remain with the company for at least twelve (12) months following course completion; otherwise, pro-rated repayment is required.

---

## Section 5: Life, Accident, and Disability Insurance
5.1 **Basic Life & AD&D Insurance**: The company provides group term life insurance and accidental death & dismemberment coverage equal to **two times (2x) the employee's base annual salary**, fully funded by the company, up to a maximum benefit of $750,000.00 USD.  
5.2 **Short-Term Disability (STD)**: Following a seven (7) day elimination period, STD replaces **66.6% of base pre-disability weekly salary for up to 26 weeks** for approved medical disability.  
5.3 **Long-Term Disability (LTD)**: For continuous disability exceeding 180 days, LTD provides **60% of base monthly salary up to $10,000.00 per month** until retirement age.

---

## Section 6: Employee Wellness, Fitness Subsidy, and Mental Health (EAP)
6.1 **Monthly Wellness and Fitness Stipend**: Employees are eligible for a taxable reimbursement of **$75.00 USD per month** for gym memberships, fitness class subscriptions, yoga studios, meditation app subscriptions (e.g. Headspace/Calm), or home fitness equipment.  
6.2 **Employee Assistance Program (EAP)**: Confidential counseling services offering up to **eight (8) free mental health therapy sessions per issue per year** for employees and their household members, available 24/7/365.
