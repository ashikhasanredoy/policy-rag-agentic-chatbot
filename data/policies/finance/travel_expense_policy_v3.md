# Business Travel, Expense Reimbursement, and Corporate Card Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-003  
**Category:** Finance  
**Department:** Global Finance, Accounting & Procurement  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Status:** Active  
**Applies To:** All Full-time, Part-time, and Contract Employees Globally  

---

## Section 1: Policy Objectives and General Principles
1.1 **Purpose**: This policy outlines the standards, authorizations, and reimbursement limits for all business travel, client entertainment, lodging, transport, and out-of-pocket corporate expenditures incurred on behalf of the company.  
1.2 **Core Principle**: All business travel must be justified by legitimate commercial necessity. Employees are expected to exercise fiscal prudence and treat company funds with the same care as their own personal finances.  
1.3 **Pre-Approval Requirement**: All business trips exceeding $1,000 in projected cost or involving international travel require written pre-authorization via the Travel Management Portal approved by the employee's Department Head at least fourteen (14) days prior to booking.

---

## Section 2: Air and Rail Transportation Standards
2.1 **Domestic Flights (Under 5 Hours)**: All domestic and short-haul international flights under five (5) consecutive hours must be booked in **Economy / Standard Coach Class**. Premium Economy is permitted only if the fare differential is under $100 or when medically certified.  
2.2 **Long-Haul International Flights (7+ Hours)**: Non-stop international flights with scheduled flight duration exceeding seven (7) continuous hours are eligible for **Business Class** seating, subject to Vice President (VP) pre-approval. Premium Economy is authorized for flights between 5 and 7 hours.  
2.3 **Advance Booking Window**: Flight reservations must be made at least fourteen (14) calendar days prior to departure through the designated Corporate Travel Agency (Navan / Concur). Bookings made within 7 days require a business justification.  
2.4 **Airline Loyalty Programs and Upgrades**: Employees may retain frequent flyer miles and loyalty points for personal use; however, choice of airline must be governed by lowest logical airfare within a 2-hour departure window. Airline upgrades at company expense are strictly prohibited.  
2.5 **Rail Travel**: Standard Class is required for rail travel under 3 hours. First Class rail travel is authorized for journeys exceeding 3 hours where working facilities are required.

---

## Section 3: Lodging and Accommodation Guidelines
3.1 **Standard Corporate Hotel Rates**: Employees must book single standard rooms at company-preferred partner hotels. Maximum allowable nightly room rates (excluding mandatory local taxes) are capped as follows:
- **Standard Tier Cities**: Up to **$200.00 USD** per night.
- **Tier-1 High-Cost Metro Cities** (New York City, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Singapore, Zurich, Paris): Up to **$280.00 USD** per night.
- **Conference / Event Hotels**: If attending an authorized corporate conference, booking the negotiated conference room rate is permissible even if it exceeds standard caps by up to 20%.  
3.2 **Non-Reimbursable Hotel Charges**: The following hotel expenses are strictly non-reimbursable: in-room pay-per-view movies, minibar alcohol snacks, spa services, laundry services (unless travel duration exceeds 5 consecutive business days), and fitness club day fees.

---

## Section 4: Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem)
4.1 **Daily Per Diem Maximum**: When traveling on official business requiring an overnight stay, employees are entitled to a daily meal reimbursement up to a combined maximum of **$75.00 USD** per day.  
4.2 **Itemized Meal Breakdown**:
- **Breakfast**: Up to **$15.00 USD** (applicable if breakfast is not included in hotel room rate or conference schedule).
- **Lunch**: Up to **$25.00 USD**.
- **Dinner**: Up to **$35.00 USD**.  
4.3 **Alcohol Policy**: Alcoholic beverages consumed during individual meals are not reimbursable under standard per diem rules.  
4.4 **Same-Day Travel Meals**: Day trips not involving an overnight stay qualify only for lunch reimbursement up to $20.00 if travel extends beyond the normal working day by at least 3 hours.

---

## Section 5: Ground Transportation and Rental Vehicles
5.1 **Taxis and Ridesharing**: Public transit, airport shuttle buses, and standard ridesharing (UberX, Lyft Standard) are the preferred methods of ground transportation. Premium vehicle categories (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) are not reimbursable.  
5.2 **Rental Cars**: Rental vehicles are authorized only when public transit or ridesharing is demonstrably impractical or more expensive (e.g. multi-site rural client visits). Approved category is **Intermediate / Compact Sedan**. SUVs and luxury cars require executive pre-approval.  
5.3 **Rental Insurance**: For domestic US rentals, employees must decline collision damage waivers (CDW/LDW) as coverage is provided through the Corporate Travel Insurance Policy. For international rentals, local mandatory CDW insurance must be accepted.  
5.4 **Personal Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement**: Use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at the standard IRS / government statutory rate (**$0.67 USD per mile for 2026**). Commuting from home to the permanent office is non-reimbursable.

---

## Section 6: Client Entertainment and Business Meals
6.1 **Eligibility**: Business meals with external clients, partners, or prospective recruits must serve a clear business purpose.  
6.2 **Spending Threshold**: Client dining is capped at **$125.00 USD per attendee** (inclusive of food, moderate alcoholic beverages, tax, and gratuity). Events exceeding this cap require Director approval.  
6.3 **Documentation**: Receipts must include an itemized invoice, business purpose, and the names, titles, and organizations of all attendees.  
6.4 **Senior Attendee Rule**: When multiple company employees attend the same business meal, the most senior employee present must pay and submit the expense report.

---

## Section 7: Expense Reporting, Documentation, and Deadlines
7.1 **Submission Window**: All travel expense reports accompanied by legible itemized receipts must be submitted via Concur within **thirty (30) calendar days** following the conclusion of the trip.  
7.2 **Late Submission & Rejection**: Expense claims submitted between 31 and 60 days require Department Head exception approval. Claims submitted **after sixty (60) days will be rejected automatically** and cannot be reimbursed.  
7.3 **Receipt Threshold**: Itemized receipts are mandatory for all individual transactions of **$25.00 USD and above**. Credit card charge slips showing only the total amount without itemization are insufficient.  
7.4 **Currency Conversion**: Foreign expenses are converted based on the official exchange rate on the date of transaction or the actual rate charged on the employee's corporate credit card statement.

---

## Section 8: Corporate Credit Cards and Cash Advances
8.1 **Corporate Card Issuance**: Employees who travel at least three (3) times per year are eligible for a Corporate American Express / Visa card with manager endorsement.  
8.2 **Prohibited Personal Charges**: Corporate cards must never be used for personal expenses under any circumstance. Incidental personal charges must be identified and settled within 7 days.  
8.3 **Emergency Cash Advances**: Cash advances are restricted to international travel to cash-dominant economies and are limited to $500.00 USD with Finance Director sign-off.
