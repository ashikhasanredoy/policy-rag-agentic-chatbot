# Customer Data Privacy, Global Compliance (GDPR/CCPA), and Data Governance Policy

**Document ID:** POL-CUST-007  
**Category:** Customer & Privacy  
**Department:** Data Privacy, Legal & Security Office  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**Status:** Active  
**Statutory Compliance:** EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), UK Data Protection Act  

---

## Section 1: Scope, Purpose, and Privacy Principles
1.1 **Purpose**: This policy sets forth the mandatory operational standards for collecting, processing, storing, transferring, and disposing of customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII).  
1.2 **Core Privacy Principles**:
- **Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency**: Data must be processed lawfully and with clear user notice.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Customer data collected for one stated purpose (e.g. order fulfillment) may not be reused for secondary purposes without affirmative opt-in consent.
- **Data Minimization**: Only the absolute minimum PII necessary for service delivery may be collected.

---

## Section 2: Data Subject Rights and Request Protocols (DSAR)
2.1 **Individual Rights**: Under GDPR and CCPA, customers have enforceable rights regarding their personal data, including:
- **Right to Access**: Right to receive a structured copy of all personal data held.
- **Right to Rectification**: Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete PII.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")**: Right to request permanent deletion of customer records, subject to statutory tax and financial retention laws.
- **Right to Data Portability**: Right to receive data in standard JSON or CSV machine-readable formats.  
2.2 **Response SLA (30 Days)**: All formal Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) submitted to `privacy@company.com` must be verified and fulfilled within **thirty (30) calendar days** of receipt.

---

## Section 3: Data Retention Schedules and Deletion Mechanisms
3.1 **Standard Customer Account Retention**: Active customer account data is retained for the duration of the commercial agreement plus **two (2) years** following account closure for audit purposes.  
3.2 **Financial and Transaction Records**: Invoices, payment transaction logs, and tax records are retained for **seven (7) years** in accordance with statutory accounting standards.  
3.3 **Application Logs and Telemetry**: Server access logs and customer telemetry streams are retained for a maximum of **ninety (90) days**, after which they are automatically expunged or anonymized.

---

## Section 4: International Data Transfers and Standard Contractual Clauses
4.1 **Cross-Border Transfers**: Customer data originating within the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK transferred to servers located outside the EEA requires executed EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and a Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA).  
4.2 **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)**: All cloud sub-processors and third-party vendors must sign our enterprise DPA guaranteeing equivalent technical and organizational security controls (TOMs).
